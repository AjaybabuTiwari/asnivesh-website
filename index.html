Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

Is Nivesh a bank or a lender?add

No. Nivesh is a personal tracking tool — it doesn't hold your money, lend it, or give financial advice. It simply helps you see your own numbers clearly, in one place.

Does the person I share an investment or Udhar entry with need the app too?add

Yes — cross-confirmation (shared investments, mutually-confirmed Udhar entries) requires both people to be on Nivesh. You can still track your own entries solo either way.

Is my data private?add

Your data is stored securely and is only visible to you and anyone you explicitly choose to share it with. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Is Nivesh free?add

Yes, Nivesh is free to use and supported by ads.
